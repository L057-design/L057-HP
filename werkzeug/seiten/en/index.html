About L057

Something Lost. Found Form.

Some things are lost before they take shape. L057 is an attempt to bring what was lost into material form. Concrete, light, 3D-printed forms and emotion become objects, designed and made in Austria. Each piece holds on to what would otherwise have remained a thought.
