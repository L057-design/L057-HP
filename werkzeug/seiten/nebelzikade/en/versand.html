Ordering

Add the pieces you want to your cart and send the order by email. Unique pieces are reserved for 7 days after your order until payment is received. You will then receive a confirmation with the payment details. The purchase is only concluded with this confirmation. Shipping costs are charged only once per order.

Size

Measure your wrist with a tape measure or a strip of paper, just above the wrist bone. Then choose the matching size. If you are between two sizes or prefer a looser fit, take the larger one. For other sizes choose “Custom” and add your measurement to the order. More under Care & size.

Shipping

Austria: from €4.90 per order, delivery in approx. 3–7 working days
EU countries: from €9.90 per order, delivery in approx. 5–10 working days
Personal handover is possible by arrangement.
Other countries: on request. Please choose “Other country” in the cart and I will send you a quote.
Delivery time starts once payment has been received. For shipments outside the EU, customs duties and import taxes may apply and are paid by you.

Packaging

Every piece comes in a small fabric pouch, safely packed in an envelope or small box.

Payment

Payment in advance by bank transfer or PayPal. You will receive the payment details with the order confirmation.
