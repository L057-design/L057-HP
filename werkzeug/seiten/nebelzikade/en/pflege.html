Care & size

So your piece fits and stays beautiful for a long time.

Finding your size

Wrap a tape measure or a strip of paper around your wrist, just above the wrist bone. Don't pull too tight. Mark where the strip meets and measure the length with a ruler.

If you like a looser fit or are between two sizes, take the larger one. For other sizes choose “Custom” and add your measurement to the order.

Care

  • Take it off before showering, swimming and sports. Water and sweat tire the elastic.
  • Let perfume and lotion dry first, then put on your jewellery.
  • Don't roll it over your hand; gently stretch it and slip it on.
  • Store pieces separately, ideally in the pouch they came in, so the stones don't scratch.
  • Silver tarnishes over time. A soft silver cloth brings back the shine.

A little stone lore

Moonstone

Milky with a bluish sheen. Long associated with the moon and the night.

Obsidian

Volcanic glass, deep black and smooth. Once used as a mirror stone.

Amethyst

Violet quartz. Its name comes from Greek and means “not intoxicated”.

Rose quartz

Pale pink quartz, a stone of love in many stories.

Aventurine

Green quartz with a fine glitter from tiny inclusions.

Labradorite

Grey with a play of blue to green that flashes depending on the light.
