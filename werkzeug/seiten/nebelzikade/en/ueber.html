How a piece is made

  1. I

    Finding the stones

    Every piece starts with the stones. Colour, sheen and small inclusions decide what it becomes.

  2. II

    Laying and stringing

    The beads are laid out, moved around and laid out again until the row feels right. Then they are strung by hand.

  3. III

    Knotting and resting

    The knot is secured and hidden. Before it travels, the piece rests for one night.
