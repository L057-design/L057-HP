General

This website presents my works and allows you to buy them or get in touch about works and collaborations. Protecting your data is important to me. Below I explain which data is processed when you visit this website.

Hosting via GitHub Pages

This website is provided via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit the website, technically necessary data (e.g. IP address, time of access, page requested, user agent) is processed in order to deliver the website and ensure its security.

Further information can be found in GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact by email

If you contact me by email, the data you provide (e.g. email address, name, content of the message) is processed in order to handle your enquiry. I do not pass this data on without a legal basis.

My mailbox is hosted by Gmail (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Emails are stored there; Google may also process data in the USA.

Cookies / tracking

This website does not use its own tracking tools (e.g. Google Analytics) and does not set marketing cookies. Technically necessary processing by the hosting provider may still take place.

Cart and orders

The contents of your cart are stored only locally in your browser (local storage) and are not transmitted to me unless you send an order. You can empty the cart at any time or delete it via your browser settings. Your language choice (German/English) is also stored only locally. When you send an order by email, I process the data provided (name, shipping address, email address, works ordered) to handle the order (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and keep invoices and accounting records in accordance with statutory retention obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR, usually 7 years).

Shipping

For delivery I pass your name and shipping address on to Österreichische Post AG. This does not apply to self-pickup.

Payment

If you pay by bank transfer, I receive the data from your transfer (name, account details, amount) via my bank. If you pay with PayPal, the data needed for the payment is transferred to PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A., 22–24 Boulevard Royal, 2449 Luxembourg. PayPal's privacy policy also applies.

Your rights

Depending on the applicable law, you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing. If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Austria: Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).
